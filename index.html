02 / Projects

ZeroVid (formerly KreatorAI)

From first idea to published content and performance insights, in one AI powered studio.

In development · AI content creation and management

What if your whole content team fit in one workspace?

ZeroVid is built for people who need a steady, engaging presence but do not have a production team. Plan, create, edit, publish, and learn from performance across LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, and X from one place.

01 / THE PROBLEM

Why is it hard to post consistently?

Ideas, scripts, design, editing, scheduling, and analytics often live in separate tools. For a solo founder or small organization, every post can become a full production job. ZeroVid brings those steps together and keeps the creative context connected.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

How does an idea become a post?

Start with an idea, turn it into a content plan, then produce the right format in the same workspace. Create with your own footage or generate assets with AI; edit video, images, text, and PDFs; then prepare and schedule posts for each platform.

03 / THE AI DIRECTOR

How does it keep content on brand?

The AI Director works with workspace context and reusable skills to shape concepts, scripts, and creative decisions. Saved knowledge helps carry your audience, goals, voice, and past content across posts, so every new piece starts with what the studio has already learned.

04 / WHO IT IS FOR

Who can use ZeroVid?

Solo founders, creators, small business owners, SaaS teams, nonprofits, and communities that want to reach an audience without building a full production team.

What can you make?

Product demos and explainers, SaaS launch content, animated stories, educational posts, community updates, campaign assets, and short form video, plus platform ready images, copy, and PDFs.

What works with your workflow?

Use your own videos or AI generated video. Bring in connectors, creative skills, avatars, and custom voices to extend the studio around the way you work.

What happens after publishing?

Manage the content calendar and review analytics in the same platform. Use previous posts and performance as context for planning what to create next.

03 / Research

ReyGen
dynamic EV charging.

Independent physics and electrical engineering research, turned into a simulator.

I studied Dynamic Wireless Power Transfer (DWPT): charging an electric vehicle while it moves over in-road induction coils. The model uses electromagnetic induction and magnetic flux (ΦB = B · A, Faraday’s Law), accounting for coupling losses and real physical constraints.

To test the idea beyond equations, I built ReyGen around real road, climate and tariff conditions along Dubai’s 29.22 km E311 Expressway (Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road). The simulator models a passenger EV traveling at highway speeds and includes a 2,000-run Monte Carlo uncertainty analysis.

The reported scenario reaches net-zero battery depletion at 12.5% road-coil coverage, and a net gain of 3.30 kWh at 25% coverage. Parameters shown in the portfolio include 75 kW SAE J2954/2 coils and 80% coupling.
